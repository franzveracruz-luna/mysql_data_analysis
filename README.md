# Olist SQL Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes the Olist e-commerce dataset using SQL to uncover actionable business insights across revenue, customer behavior, and product performance.
The objective is to demonstrate practical data analytics proficiency through structured SQL queries, logical problem-solving, data-driven analysis, and clear communication of business findings.

---

## Business Questions
1. **Q1:** Who are the top 10 customers by total amount spent?  
2. **Q2:** What is the monthly revenue trend across the dataset?  
3. **Q3:** What is the month‑over‑month change in revenue?  
4. **Q4:** Which product categories generate the most revenue?  
5. **Q5:** Which are the top 3 products within each category by revenue?  
6. **Q6:** How can customers be segmented into spend tiers (Low / Medium / High)?  
7. **Q7:** How many customers are repeat vs one‑time buyers?  
8. **Q8:** What percentage of total revenue comes from the top category?

---

## Key Findings
- **Top Customers (Q1):** Identified the 10 highest‑spending customers, showing strong concentration of revenue among a small group.  
- **Monthly Revenue (Q2):** Revealed consistent growth from 2017–2018 with seasonal peaks in November and mid‑year months.  
- **MoM Change (Q3):** Quantified monthly growth and decline patterns, highlighting promotional and holiday surges.  
- **Top Categories (Q4):** Found that a few categories dominate total revenue, driving most of the business performance.  
- **Top Products (Q5):** Ranked the top 3 products per category, showing which items contribute most to category revenue.  
- **Customer Segmentation (Q6):** Classified customers into Low, Medium, and High spend tiers for targeted marketing.  
- **Repeat Buyers (Q7):** Measured customer retention, distinguishing one‑time vs repeat buyers.  
- **Revenue Concentration (Q8):** Calculated the percentage of total revenue from the top category, revealing business dependency.

---

## Recommendations
- Focus marketing and inventory planning on **top‑performing categories and products**.  
- Develop **loyalty programs** to convert one‑time buyers into repeat customers.  
- Use **spend tier segmentation** for personalized campaigns and retention strategies.  
- Monitor **seasonal trends** to optimize promotions and logistics ahead of peak months.  
- Diversify product offerings to reduce reliance on a few dominant categories.

---

## Tools & Skills
- **SQL (MySQL):** Aggregations, CTEs, window functions, joins, CASE logic, ranking, and date formatting.  
- **Data Analysis:** Revenue trend analysis, segmentation, ranking, and percentage contribution.  
- **Business Intelligence:** Translating query results into actionable insights and recommendations.
